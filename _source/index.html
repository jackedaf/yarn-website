Why Do We Scroll?

Every time we pick up our phones, there's a feeling behind it, like boredom, anxiety or avoidance. Yarn untangles the emotional threads that cause us to reach for our phones.

About Yarn

We made Yarn as an answer to a question: what if language models could end phone addiction? One that would meet us in these moments with nothing but a few tools to help us scroll less, and a wish: that journaling would leave us more present, introspective, and sentimental.
